## Quick Facts

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Original Manga** | *CITY* by Keiichi Arawi — serialized in Kodansha's *Weekly Morning* (Sep 2016 – Feb 2021; comeback run Dec 2024 – Oct 2025), 15 volumes complete |
| **TV Anime** | *CITY THE ANIMATION* — 13 episodes (80 segments), aired July 7 – September 29, 2025 (Tokyo MX, ABC TV, TV Aichi, BS11, AT-X) |
| **Animation Studio** | Kyoto Animation (studio's first non-sequel TV series in six years) |
| **Core Staff** | Director: Taichi Ishidate · Character Design & Chief Animation Director: Tamami Tokuyama · Art Director: Shiori Yamazaki · Color Design: Kana Miyata · 3D Director: Tatsunori Kase · Sound Director: Yota Tsuruoka · Music: Piranhans |
| **Theme Songs** | OP: `"Hello"` by Furui Riho · ED: `"LUCKY"` by TOMOO |
| **Sister Work** | [*Nichijou* (日常)](https://github.com/chius-me/awesome-nichijou) by Keiichi Arawi & Kyoto Animation |

## Animation Craft, Interviews and Criticism

### Sakuga & Production Notes

- [Sakugabooru — `city_the_animation` Tag](https://www.sakugabooru.com/post?tags=city_the_animation) - Indexed collection of key animation cuts showcasing Taichi Ishidate's analog-first visual experimentation and background animation.
- [Sakuga Blog — *From Nichijou to CITY: Kyoto Animation's Gentle Touch Adapting Keiichi Arawi's Madness*](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2024/09/21/from-nichijou-to-city-kyoto-animations-gentle-touch-adapting-keiichi-arawis-madness/) - Pre-broadcast essay examining the 14-year evolution connecting *Nichijou* and *CITY*.
- [Sakuga Blog — *CITY The Animation Production Notes 01*](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2025/07/07/city-the-animation-production-notes-01/) - Deep dive into the close collaboration between Keiichi Arawi and KyoAni's in-house staff, simultaneity, and Shiori Yamazaki's bold art direction.
- [Sakuga Blog — *CITY: The Animation Production Notes #02–11*](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2025/09/20/city-the-animation-production-notes-02-11/) - Mid-series analysis of how the show defies modern TV animation workflows and visual norms.
- [Sakuga Blog — *Final Production Notes And Kyoto Animation's Future*](https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2025/09/29/city-the-animation-final-production-notes-and-kyoto-animations-future/) - Post-finale reflection on KyoAni's analog training philosophy, the finale musical sequence, and what *CITY* means for the studio's next era.

### Interviews & Special Issues

- [*Eureka* (ユリイカ) Sept 2025 — Keiichi Arawi Special Issue (Seidosha)](https://www.seidosha.co.jp/book/4054.html) - Essential Japanese monograph (*ISBN 978-4-7917-0466-8*) featuring a dialogue between **Keiichi Arawi and Director Taichi Ishidate** alongside a contribution from Mikako Komatsu (Midori Nagumo).
- [Animate Times — Director Taichi Ishidate Interview](https://www.animatetimes.com/news/details.php?id=1755044912) - In-depth Japanese interview on crafting "fun," "cute," and "kind" fiction in *CITY THE ANIMATION*.
- [MANTANWEB — Director Taichi Ishidate Interview](https://mantan-web.jp/article/20250726dog00m200050000c.html) - Interview discussing the distinction between gag and comedy and the team's painstaking visual details.
- [Billboard — Furui Riho Talks `"Hello"`](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/furui-riho-hello-interview-theme-song-city-the-animation-1236055103/) - English translation of Billboard JAPAN's interview on translating the manga's bustling ensemble energy into music.

### Reviews & Podcasts

- [Aftermath — *City The Animation Turns Every Single Episode Into A Visual Flex*](https://aftermath.site/city-the-animation-review-anime-nichijou-kyoto-animation/) - Enthusiastic critical review highlighting split-screen simultaneity and hand-drawn picture-book aesthetics.
- [Animation World Network — *Kyoto Animation Does Sketch Comedy: 'Nichijou' and 'City'*](https://www.awn.com/animationworld/kyoto-animation-does-sketch-comedy-nichijou-and-city) - Comparative piece examining KyoAni's sketch-comedy craftsmanship across both series.
- [Mangasplaining — Episode 35: *CITY* Vol. 1](https://www.mangasplaining.com/blog/ep-35-city-vol-1-by-keiichi-arawi) - English manga podcast episode unpacking Keiichi Arawi's paneling, slapstick timing, and character ensembles.
